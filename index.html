How it works

😊
Smile and flowers bloom around you
🌸
Swipe the flowers at the bottom and tap one to switch
⏺

📷
The camera button takes a photo
⏱
The timer adds a 3 or 10 second countdown (tap it again to cancel)
💾
To keep it: photos - tap Save Image; videos - tap Save to Photos, then Save Video
